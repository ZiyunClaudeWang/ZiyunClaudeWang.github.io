Ziyun (Claude) Wang

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, where I lead the SPIKE Lab. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, and neuromorphic computing, with a focus on event-based, continuous-time perception—building systems that see and act at the speed of the physical world. I received my Ph.D. from the GRASP Laboratory at the University of Pennsylvania, where I was advised by Kostas Daniilidis.
